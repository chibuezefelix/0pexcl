OPXL Limited  ·  Global Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective as of 22 May 2026  ·  Last updated 22 May 2026

OPXL Limited and its affiliates ("OPXL," "we," "us," or "our") build enterprise software, fintech infrastructure, mobile applications, cloud services, and artificial intelligence solutions used by individuals and businesses worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, transfer, store, retain, and otherwise process Personal Information across all the jurisdictions in which our Services are made available.

This Policy applies globally and is supplemented by jurisdiction-specific provisions where local law grants you additional rights — including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), the Brazilian Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the Australian Privacy Act 1988, and other applicable data protection laws.

By accessing, browsing, or using our website, applications, or Services, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

In this Policy, "Personal Information" (also referred to as "personal data" in some jurisdictions) means any information that identifies or makes identifiable a natural person. This includes the meaning given to "personal data" under Article 4(1) of the GDPR and UK GDPR, "personal information" under the CCPA/CPRA, "personal data" under the NDPA, "dados pessoais" under the LGPD, and equivalent definitions under other applicable laws.

OPXL Limited is the data controller in respect of Personal Information we collect about our customers, website visitors, and end-users of our consumer products. Where we process Personal Information on behalf of business customers (for example, in connection with payment processing or enterprise software services), OPXL acts as a data processor, and the business customer is the data controller.

  • Business Information. If you are a business customer: business registration details (such as your CAC certificate, certificate of incorporation, articles of association, or local equivalent), business address, names and identification of directors and beneficial owners, and other corporate information required by applicable law.
  • Sensitive Information. Where you provide it and we are lawfully permitted to process it, we may collect special categories of Personal Information such as government identifiers, biometric data, or — only where strictly necessary for a specific Service — health information.

Information We Collect from Other Sources

  • Identity Verification Providers. Information from third-party identity verification services, credit bureaux, financial institutions, national identity databases (where lawful), and publicly available sources.
  • Compliance and Fraud Sources. Information used for sanctions screening, anti-money laundering (AML) checks, fraud detection, and adverse media screening.

03Lawful Bases for Processing

Where required by applicable law (including the GDPR, UK GDPR, NDPA, LGPD, and POPIA), we process your Personal Information only where we have a lawful basis to do so. We rely on one or more of the following bases:

  • Consent — where you have given specific, informed, and unambiguous consent for a particular purpose. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to perform a contract with you or to take steps at your request before entering into one.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation to which we are subject (for example, KYC, AML, sanctions, tax reporting).
  • Vital interests — where processing is necessary to protect your vital interests or those of another natural person.
  • Public interest — where processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest.
  • Legitimate interests — where processing is necessary for our or a third party's legitimate interests, except where such interests are overridden by your interests, rights, or freedoms. Our legitimate interests typically include providing and improving our Services, securing our systems, preventing fraud, and promoting our business.

05International Data Transfers

Because OPXL operates globally and uses service providers based in multiple countries, your Personal Information may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from countries other than the one in which you reside. The data-protection laws of those countries may differ from the laws of your country.

Where we transfer Personal Information across borders, we implement appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law, including one or more of the following mechanisms:

  • Adequacy decisions — transfers to countries that the relevant authority (such as the European Commission, the UK ICO, or the Nigeria Data Protection Commission) has determined to provide an adequate level of protection;
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) — including the EU SCCs (Commission Decision 2021/914), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or Addendum, and equivalent contractual mechanisms approved by other regulators;
  • Binding Corporate Rules where applicable;
  • Your explicit consent to the transfer after being informed of the possible risks;
  • Necessity for the performance of a contract between you and us.

For EU/UK/Swiss data subjects, we additionally rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where Personal Information is transferred to certified entities in the United States. Copies of our standard transfer documents are available on request.

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights in relation to your Personal Information. Some rights apply only in certain jurisdictions, while others are recognised as a global baseline.

  • The right to be informed about the collection and use of your Personal Information.
  • The right of access to the Personal Information we hold about you.
  • The right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Information.
  • The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") in certain circumstances.
  • The right to restrict processing in certain circumstances.

  • The right to data portability — to receive your Personal Information in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 14. 08How We Store and Protect Your Information

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Information against unauthorised or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage. These measures include encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest where appropriate, access controls based on the principle of least privilege, network segmentation, security monitoring, regular vulnerability and penetration testing, employee training, and contractual safeguards with our service providers.

Where applicable, we comply with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) for the handling of payment-card information. We pursue industry-recognised security certifications (such as ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2) where appropriate to our Services.

Despite our safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We will notify you and competent supervisory authorities of any Personal Information breach in accordance with applicable law.

09How Long We Retain Your Information

We retain Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, regulatory, or reporting requirements. The criteria we use to determine retention periods include:

  • the duration of our ongoing relationship with you;
  • any legal obligation to retain the data (for example, minimum five (5) to seven (7) years for KYC, financial, and AML records under most jurisdictions in which we operate);
  • statutes of limitation under applicable law;
  • ongoing or potential disputes; and

When Personal Information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it.
